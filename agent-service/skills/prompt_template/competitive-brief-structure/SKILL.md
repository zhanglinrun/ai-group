## Template Body

```text
# Executive Summary
- One-paragraph thesis with evidence ids.

## Feature Comparison
- Winning strengths
- Weaknesses / gaps

## Pricing Signals
- Plan tiers
- Packaging differences

## User Feedback Signals
- Positive trend
- Negative trend

## Recommendations
- Tactical actions (1-2 weeks)
- Strategic actions (1-2 quarters)
```

## Why

This template keeps reports scannable while preserving evidence grounding discipline.
